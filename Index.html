Help

Getting started
Open Input, press Start audio and plug in your guitar (use headphones to avoid feedback). Then choose File → New Orbit and pick Circle, Sphere or Galaxy.

Adding effects
Click an icon in the left toolbar, then click inside the circle to place it. You can place the same effect as many times as you like.

Intensity
Distance from the center. Center = no effect (clean guitar), edge = maximum. Overdrive and Distortion stop at 40%, Sustain at 50%. On a sphere, intensity follows depth instead: front = maximum, back = minimum.

Sphere
The effects sit on a 3D wireframe sphere. Drag an empty part of the sphere to rotate it: effects facing you get stronger and effects turning to the back fade out, while their stereo position and brightness change too. Effects on the back are dimmed and quiet but still work. The Reset position button (bottom right) brings the sphere back to its starting position.

Galaxy
A rotating cloud of stars. Each effect attaches to a star: click or drag near the star you want and the effect snaps to it. Drag empty space to rotate it. Zoom with the mouse wheel, a pinch or the +/− buttons: zooming out shrinks the galaxy so effects move toward the center and weaken, zooming in pushes them toward the rim (past the rim they stay at maximum). Perspective makes the stars near you spread apart, so rotating changes how the effects relate to each other. In a galaxy every effect also feeds a cosmic space, a huge reverb plus drifting echo loops that get longer and more psychedelic the stronger the effect, stretching toward infinity. Reset position restores both rotation and zoom.

Stereo position
Effects on the left half are heard more on the left, effects on the right half more on the right.

Brightness
The top of the circle makes an effect brighter, the bottom makes it darker.

Volume handle
Drag the small handle next to an effect up or down to change its level. Double-click it to reset to 100%.

Moving and removing
Drag an effect to move it, arrow keys nudge it. Double-click it or press Delete to remove it.

Effects
Overdrive: fuzz. Distortion: thick fuzz with an octave edge. Sustain: long, singing notes. Chorus, Phaser, Flanger, Tremolo, Wah. Delay: slows down toward the edge. Doppler Ping-Pong: echoes that bounce left and right with pitch sweeps. Reverb.

Equalizer
Five bands from 80 Hz to 8 kHz, ±12 dB, applied to the final sound.

Animate
On a circle, effects orbit the center, pulse in and out and drift randomly. On a sphere or galaxy, the whole space spins and Random drift makes it wobble. Pulse tilts a sphere back and forth, and on a galaxy it breathes the zoom in and out. Speed controls all the movement.

Recording
● records the final output, ▶ plays it back, ■ stops, ↓ downloads the take.

Presets and keys
File → Save preset / Open preset keeps your circle. Ctrl+N creates a new file, Esc cancels placing an effect.
